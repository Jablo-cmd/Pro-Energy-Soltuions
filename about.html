About us

Energy specialists who focus on what your property actually needs.

Pro Energy Solutions is based in Rosettenville, Johannesburg, and designs and installs solar, battery storage and electrical systems for homes and businesses. We work directly with clients — from homeowners in Limpopo to a restaurant owner in Melville — to build systems around real usage, not a standard package.

Our approach and who we work with

How we approach every project

We don't start with equipment — we start with your property, your usage and your budget. That means site assessments before quotes, and recommendations sized to what you actually need rather than what sells the biggest system.

  • Site assessment before any recommendation
  • Clear, direct communication about cost and timeline
  • Installation and commissioning handled by our own team
  • Support available after the system is switched on

Who we work with

Homeowners, restaurant and SME owners, commercial property managers and businesses who need power they can rely on. Our completed projects span residential rooftops, a restaurant kitchen, and commercial-scale battery storage rooms.

  • Homeowners
  • Restaurants & hospitality businesses
  • SMEs and commercial property owners
  • Facilities and property managers
