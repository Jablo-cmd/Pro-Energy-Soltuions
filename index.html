Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does a solar system cost?

Cost depends on your energy consumption, property requirements, equipment choice and system design. We provide a clear quote after assessing your specific needs rather than quoting a generic package price.

How do I know what size solar system I need?

We start with an assessment of your consumption and usage patterns, then size the system to match — rather than guessing or defaulting to the largest option.

Can solar help during power outages?

Yes, when paired with battery storage configured for your essential loads. We'll explain what a system can and can't cover during an outage before you commit.

Can you install solar for a business?

Yes — we've installed systems for a restaurant and other SMEs. Commercial installations are assessed the same way as residential ones: around your actual operating needs.
